Localization comparison withAprilVINS

Rows are manipulation tasks and columns are localization methods. The clips summarize the qualitative trajectory comparisons behind the paper tables, showing each estimated trajectory against the recorded TCP reference. Fail denotes no evaluable trajectory; unavailable entries explain recording, input-processing, or evaluation issues. Metrics are APE RMSE (m) / time completion. Each row shares one playback timeline, with a scene recording in the task column. Separate scene and sensor recordings are synchronized by normalized reference-trajectory progress, not by absolute time. For Box 02 and Pick-and-place 01–03, outside an estimate's recorded time range, its video holds the boundary frame with the complete GT reference still visible.
