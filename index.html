Sensor setup

This section will summarize the recommended camera, IMU, robot TCP, and calibration layout for reproducing the MILD-style acquisition setup.

Insta360 X5 panoramic route

Front/back fisheye streams and IMU are mounted near the gripper and evaluated against the robot TCP trajectory.

  • Wide-FOV visual--inertial route for AprilVINS and VIO baselines.
  • Release assets include camera/IMU calibration and sensor-to-TCP metadata.

Insight9 stereo companion route

Left/right grayscale views provide a compact stereo route for sensor-route comparison and diagnostic localization baselines.

  • Used to study sensor layout effects under matched manipulation tasks.
  • Release assets include stereo calibration and hand--eye metadata where available.

Coordinate colors follow the paper convention: x is red, y is green, and z is blue. The diagrams are compact frame-layout illustrations; frozen numeric transforms will be released with the calibration package.
